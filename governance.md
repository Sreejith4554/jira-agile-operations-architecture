# Jira Governance Principles

1. **Design fields for decisions, not decoration.** Every required field should support routing, prioritisation, reporting or traceability.
2. **Keep workflow states meaningful.** Avoid duplicate states with no management value.
3. **Blocked must be actionable.** A blocked item needs a reason, owner and review date.
4. **Dependencies need timing.** Track both provider and consumer, plus need-by date.
5. **Dashboards should answer management questions.** What is late? What is blocked? What needs a decision? What changed?
6. **Automation should assist ownership, not obscure it.** Automated updates must be understandable and reversible.
